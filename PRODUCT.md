# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who install tarjim from GitHub on their own computer: viewers who want to understand a clip they are watching in the browser, creators who want their video subtitled in another language, and anyone holding a video or audio file on their device. They open tarjim in the middle of watching or editing, want the job started in a few seconds, then go back to what they were doing while it runs.

## Product Purpose

tarjim turns any video, from a link or a file on the device, into professional subtitles in the language the person chooses, timed to the speech and aware of who is speaking, burned into the video or saved as a subtitle file. Success is a person starting a translation from the browser in under ten seconds and getting subtitles that appear exactly when people speak.

## Brand Personality

Calm, professional, precise. The quiet confidence of a subtitling studio: the video and the translation are the stars, the tool steps aside. Copy is short, plain and friendly; Arabic copy is simple Saudi Arabic.

## Anti-references

No noisy consumer app with badges and confetti, no dashboard of cards and big numbers, no neon "AI" look, no emoji, no gradient text. Nothing that competes with the video the person is watching.

## Design Principles

1. Start in one step: the most common path (this clip, my language, burned in) is one click away.
2. Show progress honestly: stages as they really happen, clear reasons when something fails, never a fake percentage.
3. The work speaks: timing and translation quality are the product; the interface stays quiet around them.
4. Every language is first class: right-to-left and left-to-right, any script, the person's own language name shown in its own script.
5. Private by default: everything runs on the person's device except the calls they chose to allow.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.2 AA. Full keyboard use, visible focus, labels on every control, status changes announced to screen readers, respects reduced motion, works right-to-left and left-to-right.
